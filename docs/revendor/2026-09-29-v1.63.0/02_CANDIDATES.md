# Candidates (BankLedger)

One new surface, in `LibKa0s-Slash-1.0` minor 17: the shared `profile` verb (the `profiles`
descriptor field, `CliProfile`, `ProfileSwitch`, `lib.ProfileNames`, the nine `PROFILE_*` strings).

It is not offered through the adoption interview. It is the planned item SP-BL-02 of
`Ka0sAddonsCommonTasks/docs/2026-09-29-SMOKE_REWORK_AND_PROFILE_VERB/` (spec S3, owner decisions D1
and D3), and profiles exist in this addon since SP-BL-01.
